About

Technical. Aware. Always working.

I'm Hariharan Karthik, a young footballer from Bangalore. I play as a Right Back and can also cover Left Back and Defensive Midfield. From the KSFA Youth Premier League U-17 with BBFS to senior football with Snipers FC through the BDFA C, B and A Divisions, including a promotion-winning season in the B Division, I've learned the game at every level. I'm now playing senior football with Jai Bharath Nagar FC.

I'm a technical player with real precision in my passing and ball deliveries. I bring strong stamina and a high work rate, and I use my head: I read the game, spot free space early and anticipate play, so I'm already moving before the ball arrives.

I'm also a penalty taker. I stay calm and composed under pressure and have a very high conversion rate from the spot. Leadership is another strength: I've captained my team, leading by example and taking responsibility on the pitch. I'm focused on steady growth and earning a place in a squad that wants to win.
